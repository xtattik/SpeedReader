Speed Reader — Help

Speed Reader uses RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation). Words flash at a fixed focal point so your eyes never have to move across the page — only your brain does the work.

The red letter is the anchor — keep your eyes fixed on it and let the words come to you.

Getting Started

  1. Drop a file onto the upload area, browse for one, or paste text directly.
  2. Press Space or the Play button.
  3. Adjust speed with the slider — start slow and work up.
